Independent wheel conditions

Gas starts near 20 C and cools during XPBD inflation/volume expansion; tread heat does not instantly become gas heat. The runtime thermal and ignition numbers are provisional, not measured tyre-compound properties.

Implementation boundary

  • Live Drive runs tyres at 240 Hz and gas at 60 Hz. World contact samples sweep the source path; no smoke lattice is allocated at spawn.
  • Current transport is 32 cubed over 24 m: 0.75 m cells. This proves motion/ownership, not tyre-scale visual detail or performance parity with Unreal.
  • Sliding heat excludes static support, normal-impact work, rolling loss and brake-disc work. Fire requires authored sustained overheat plus remaining rubber, never randomness.
  • Drive obstruction uses actual terrain queries plus a conservative authored chassis-box proxy. Exact tyre-SDF smoke obstruction and SDF deformation are not added.
  • Fine-resolution GPU upload/rendering, calibrated smoke onset/hysteresis, material wear and calibrated fire appearance are still open milestones.
  • Existing hard-landing energy-accounting failures are not repaired or hidden by this work.

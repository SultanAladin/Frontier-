Measured diagnoses

Brake torque must oppose rotor spin, brake power must be non-positive, and idle gearbox torque is zero while brakes are held. The coasting/braking solve now couples each rolling rotor, bounded disc torque, chassis response and Coulomb road impulse. It has no wheel-lock speed gate, does not count rotor torque twice on the chassis, and uses the effective-mass metric for the friction cone. Powered Magic-Formula traction/cornering are preserved. No pose/velocity clamps or extra damping are used.

The previous physical bead width used the tread shoulders. The authored-rim surface distance was 44.619 mm. Shared rim-seat geometry reduces it to 0.379 mm of polygon faceting. The actual pressure-surface seats and published closed native envelope are measured at the recorded 20 Hz poses. Maximum native bead separation is 0.0006 mm; native tread-to-solved-particle mismatch is 0.0011 mm. The diagnostic uses the native wheel emitter and authored rigid rim, rather than an open tread tube or cylinder proxy. Tread squash is allowed; bead detachment is not.

Across both powered contour headings, the maximum drive-phase uphill-positive displacement is -0.1448 m: no propulsive uphill sign reversal was measured. World Z is decomposed independently into grade travel and suspension-normal movement.

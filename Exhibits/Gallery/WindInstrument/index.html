The anemometer, drawn by the engine

Every pixel below came out of a real ImGui draw list, rasterised on the CPU and written to PNG by Exhibits/Workbench/Wind/NativeWindInstrument.cpp. Nothing here is a mock-up.

The source is InspectorDepot/panels/wind.js — the panel the shipped bundle actually mounts. InspectorHost.js renders the depot panels and overrides only the light family with LightPanel.js, which is why lights were the wrong half and wind is not. The trace is then rewritten at build time by InstrumentSpecification.js: the green gust band becomes a neutral wash under the trace, the stroke drops to rgba(210,210,210,.36), and the samples that sit above the mean are brightened in place rather than drawn as a second trace. Both the peak and trough dots (#eeeeee and #d69a54) come from that rewrite.

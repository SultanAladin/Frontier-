XPBD Tyre Lab

Theory under test: physics never touches the render mesh. CPU XPBD runs a coarse lattice (default 4 rings × 48 = 192 nodes/tyre); the GPU skins a 18 432-vertex/tyre render mesh from those nodes in the vertex shader. Presets let you A/B the old design (9×128 lattice, 8 substeps × 4 sweeps) against the new one and read the frame times yourself.
